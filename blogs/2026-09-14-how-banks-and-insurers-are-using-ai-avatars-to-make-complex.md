Key Takeaways Mortgage disclosures, insurance riders, and other financial documents can remain difficult to understand even after several readings. AI avatars are one format financial-services organizations are exploring to make this information easier to understand. This matters because complex financial products actually need to stick with people, not just get clicked through.
